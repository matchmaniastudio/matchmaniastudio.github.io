Delete your Matchmania account

Matchmania Studio · last updated 30 October 2026

You can delete your Matchmania account and the data stored with it at any time — in the game, or on this page without installing anything. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

In the game

  1. Open Settings and tap Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete.

On this page

For accounts created with an email address and password.

Forgot your password? Enter your email above and tap Forgot password? — we will email you a link to set a new one, then come back here.

Can't use the form? For example, if your account has no password, or you no longer have access to it: email matchmania.studio@gmail.com from the address your account uses, with the subject “Delete my Matchmania account”. Writing from that address is how we confirm the account is yours. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Never created an account? Then there is nothing to delete here: everything the game stores lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall it.

What is deleted

  • Your account: its email address, password (which we never see — it is stored by our sign-in provider, Google Firebase, only as a one-way hash) and account ID.
  • Any progress or other data stored online under your account.

What is kept

  • Progress on your device stays until you uninstall the game — the game keeps working without an account.
  • Purchase records are held by Google Play or the App Store, not by us, under their own policies.
  • Analytics and crash reports are not linked to your account and expire on their normal schedule: up to 14 months for analytics, 90 days for crash reports. Our own copy of them is deleted on request — see the privacy policy.
