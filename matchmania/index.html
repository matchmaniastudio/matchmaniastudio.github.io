Privacy Policy — Matchmania

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Matchmania (“the app”) is developed and published by Matchmania Studio (“we”, “us”), an independent developer based in Ukraine. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

It applies to the Android and iOS versions of Matchmania, package identifier com.matchmania.game.

Contact: matchmania.studio@gmail.com

Summary

  • The app has no accounts and no login. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • The game does generate one random ID for your installation, so that lost progress can be restored. It identifies a copy of the game, not a person, and no other app can read it.
  • Analytics is optional. You are asked once, on first launch, before anything is sent. If you decline, no analytics data leaves your device.
  • Crash reporting runs whether or not you accept analytics. It exists to fix the app, not to measure you. This is explained again below, because it is the one thing that is not covered by your answer.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device only.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone other than the service providers listed below.

What the app collects

1. Analytics — only if you accept

On first launch the app asks whether you consent to analytics. Nothing is collected until you answer, and the answer is remembered. If you decline, this entire section does not apply to you.

If you accept, we use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played. It collects:

  • an app-instance identifier — a random ID generated for your installation, which identifies that installation only and is not shared with other apps on your device;
  • your device model, operating system version, language, and approximate country;
  • in-game events: levels started and completed, progression, in-game currency earned and spent, and screens viewed;
  • the app version and build number of your installation.

We use this only in aggregate, to see which levels are too hard, where players stop playing, and whether the game’s economy is balanced. We do not use it to build advertising profiles, and the app currently shows no advertisements.

The app does not collect your device’s Advertising ID. Analytics is configured with ad identifier collection switched off, and the app does not request the AD_ID permission, so nothing here can be linked to your activity in other apps.

2. Crash reporting — always active

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to detect and diagnose crashes. This runs regardless of your analytics choice, because it is what allows a broken build to be repaired rather than a way of measuring you. It collects, only when the app crashes or hits an error:

  • the stack trace and the sequence of actions leading to the crash;
  • the app version, build number, source commit, and release channel;
  • your device model, operating system version, and device state at the moment of failure (available memory and storage, orientation);
  • a Crashlytics installation identifier, which is separate from the analytics identifier and is not used for measurement;
  • your installation ID — see below.

Crash reports are not linked to your gameplay history and carry no advertising identifier.

Your installation ID

When you first open the game it generates a random ID for your installation. We use it for one thing: if you contact us because your progress was lost, it is the only way to find it. The game has no accounts, so there is nothing else that could identify your save.

Two things about it worth stating plainly:

  • It is not derived from your device, your phone number, or any account. It is a random value, and it is unique to Matchmania — no other app can see it or produce the same value.
  • It is designed to survive reinstalling the game, so that progress can be restored. On iPhone and iPad it is kept in the system keychain, which is not erased when an app is deleted; on Android it is included in Google’s app backup if you have backup enabled. Erasing your device removes it.

This ID is attached to crash reports, which means it is sent whether or not you accepted analytics. If you would rather not have it at all: on iOS, deleting the app and erasing your device removes it; on Android, turning off Google backup and reinstalling generates a new one. Doing so also means we can no longer restore lost progress for you.

3. App configuration — always active

The app uses Google Firebase Remote Config to read its own settings on launch — which features are enabled, and balance values. This request is made before you answer the consent question and is not covered by it, because the app needs its own configuration in order to run at all.

This request carries a Firebase Installations ID. It is not used for analytics or advertising. If this request fails or is blocked, the app falls back to the settings shipped inside it and continues to work normally.

4. Purchases

If in-app purchases are available in your version of the app, payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or receive your card details, billing address, or payment credentials. We receive only the store’s confirmation that a purchase completed, so that we can deliver the item.

5. Data stored on your device

Your progress, settings, and in-game currency balances are saved in a file on your device. This file is not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • Your contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera.
  • Your precise location. Approximate country is derived from your IP address by Google and is available to us only at country level.
  • Your device’s Advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS), or any other identifier shared across apps. The app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS, because it does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Health, financial, or biometric data.

The app contains no advertising SDKs and no social login.

Who receives the data

Google LLC, as our service provider, operates Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config on our behalf. Their handling of this data is governed by the Firebase Privacy and Security policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play and Apple process purchases under their own privacy policies.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party.

Data is processed on Google’s infrastructure, which may be located outside your country, including in the United States. Google’s transfer safeguards are described in the policies linked above.

Your choices and rights

Change your analytics answer at any time. Open Settings in the game and use the Analytics toggle. Turning it off stops collection immediately. It is exactly as easy to turn off as it was to turn on, and no part of the game is withheld if you decline.

Delete your local data by uninstalling the app.

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or another region with equivalent law, you also have the right to request access to your data, its correction or erasure, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. To exercise any of these, email matchmania.studio@gmail.com. Because the app has no accounts, we may need your installation ID in order to find any data at all; we will explain how to read it off your screen if you ask.

Children

Matchmania is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects — for example if advertising is added — this page will be updated and the date at the top changed before the new version of the app is released. Material changes to how analytics works will be re-asked in the app rather than only posted here.
